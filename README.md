# 🎬 Amazon Prime Video Dashboard – Power BI Project

This repository contains an interactive **Power BI dashboard** that visualizes key insights into the content library of **Amazon Prime Video**. The goal is to provide a comprehensive overview of the platform’s catalog of movies and TV shows from various perspectives including content type, age ratings, genre distribution, country of origin, and release trends.

![Dashboard Screenshot](./Screenshot%202025-06-28%20112259.png)

---

## 📈 Dashboard Highlights

### ✅ KPIs
- **Total Titles:** 9,655  
- **Total Ratings:** 25  
- **Total Genres:** 519  
- **Total Directors:** 5,771  
- **Time Span:** 1920 – 2021  

### 📊 Visualized Metrics
- **Age Rating by Total Shows:** Breakdown of titles by content rating (e.g., 13+, 16+, PG-13, etc.)
- **Genres by Total Shows:** Most common genre combinations (e.g., Drama, Comedy, Action-Drama)
- **Total Shows by Country:** Geographic distribution of content production shown on a world map
- **Movies vs TV Shows:** Pie chart comparing the volume of movies (19.18%) and TV shows (80.82%)
- **Total Shows by Release Year:** Time-series showing the growth of content over the years, with a notable spike post-2000

---

## 🔧 Tools & Technologies Used

- **Power BI Desktop**
- **Bing Maps (for geo-visualization)**

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- **Drama** and **Comedy** are the dominant genres, with Drama alone accounting for nearly 1,000 titles.
- Content for **13+** and **16+** age groups forms the majority, showing a focus on teen and young adult audiences.
- The **United States**, **India**, and **United Kingdom** are leading countries in terms of content volume.
- There has been a sharp increase in the number of shows released annually since the early 2000s, indicating rapid platform expansion.

---

## 📁 Dataset

- **Source**: Public dataset from [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/shivamb/amazon-prime-movies-and-tv-shows) or similar OTT content aggregators.
- **Fields Used**: Title, Country, Genre, Age Rating, Type, Release Year, Director, Duration
